About Me

I am a senior frontend engineer who partners with design, content, and backend teams to ship inclusive digital products.

Professional Background

Over the past decade I have led interface architecture for SaaS platforms, marketing sites, and design systems. My workflow centers on semantic markup first, then layered CSS and JavaScript enhancements that respect user preferences.

Core Skills

Frontend Engineering

  • Semantic HTML5 and strict document outline discipline
  • Modern CSS layout (Grid, Flexbox) with progressive enhancement
  • TypeScript, React, and component-driven architecture

Accessibility & Quality

  • WCAG 2.2 AA conformance testing with assistive technologies
  • ARIA authoring practices and landmark navigation patterns
  • Automated and manual audit workflows (axe, Lighthouse, screen readers)

Working Principles

I believe the most maintainable interfaces are built from standards-compliant HTML, explicit labels, and keyboard-first interactions—never as an afterthought.
